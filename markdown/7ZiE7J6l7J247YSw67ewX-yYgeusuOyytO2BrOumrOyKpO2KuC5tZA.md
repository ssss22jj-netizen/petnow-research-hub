## Quick Intercept Interview — Shelter & Rescue Staff

*Goal: 2–3 minutes per person. Capture answers in shorthand. If the conversation flows, end with the follow-up ask.*

**Before you start, jot down:** organization name / person's role / shelter or rescue / rough size (animals per year, if easy to ask)

### Questions

1. **Current tools** — "What software or system do you use to run your day-to-day operations — intake, adoptions, records?"
   *(If they say spreadsheets / paper / nothing, note that — it's a signal, not a dead end.)*

2. **Spend** — "Roughly how much do you spend on it — monthly, yearly, or per adoption?"

3. **Satisfaction** — "Overall, how happy are you with it — say on a scale of 1 to 10?"

4. **Branch by answer:**
   - If satisfied (7+): "What's the one thing it does really well for you?"
   - If not satisfied (6 or below): "What's the most critical problem with it?"

5. **Willingness to switch** — "If a better solution came along, would you actually consider switching?"

6. **If no / can't switch:** "What makes switching hard for you?"
   *(Contract? Data migration? Training? 'Not worth the hassle'? — capture their words.)*

### Follow-up ask (only if the conversation went well)

> "This was really helpful. We're building something for shelters and rescues — would you be open to a 30-minute call to go deeper? What's the best email to reach you?"

**Capture:** name / email / org — and one line on what stood out from their answers.

- 순서보다 대화 흐름 우선. 6개 다 못 물어도 1·3·4(현재 툴, 만족도, 이유)가 코어
- 답변은 그들의 표현 그대로 메모 (고객 언어 확보가 Track 1·2 공통 목적)
