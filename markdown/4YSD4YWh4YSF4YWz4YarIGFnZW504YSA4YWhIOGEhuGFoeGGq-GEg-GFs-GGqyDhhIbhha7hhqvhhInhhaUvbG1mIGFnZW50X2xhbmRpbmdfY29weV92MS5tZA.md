## A. H-A — 준비 상태 판단 (ICE 400 · **1순위**) 🆕 v1 신규

**타겟**: 대표(다이앤) · 시스템 실무자(조) · 콜드 채널 전체
**이 광고의 진짜 목적**: 메시지 테스트가 아니라 **시장 크기 테스트**다. domestic shelter가 반응하면 β 성립, 반응 없으면 크로스보더 니치.

### 랜딩

> # Which dogs are ready to go?
>
> ## If you can't answer that today, some of them have been waiting for years.
>
> Every rescue has them. Dogs that could be listed, fostered, or moved — but nobody can say for sure whether they're ready. The health record is in one place. The behavior notes are with someone who saw the dog last month. The photos are on a phone. The paperwork is in an email thread.
>
> So the dog waits. Not because anything is wrong with the dog.
>
> Petnow pulls health, behavior, photos, video, and paperwork under one animal, and tells you one thing: **what's still missing before this dog can move.**
>
> - One line per animal: what's next, who owns it, what's blocking it
> - Legal, medical, behavior, location, and adoption status tracked separately — they move at different speeds
> - Works across languages and locations, so a team that can't see the dog in person still knows its status
> - Publishes to your existing sites when the dog is actually ready
>
> **The outcome we're aiming at: dogs adopted faster.**
>
> ### Book a 30-min call
> We'll ask you to pick one dog that's been waiting the longest, and walk us through why. No pitch.

### 광고 카피

| 버전 | 카피 | 채널 |
|---|---|---|
| A-1 | "Which of your dogs are ready to be listed right now? If the honest answer is 'I'd have to check with three people' — we'd like to talk." | LinkedIn, Facebook |
| A-2 | "The dog isn't the reason it's still waiting. The missing photo is." | Facebook, Reddit |
| A-3 | "Some dogs wait years in the system. Not because nobody wants them — because nobody could confirm they were ready." | LinkedIn |
| ~~A-4~~ | ⛔ **집행 제외 (크로스보더 전용)** | → `crossborder_reference.md` §5에 보존 |

> **v1.1 변경**: 크로스보더를 ICP에서 뺐으므로 A-4는 집행하지 않는다. **로컬 공통성 판정이 이 광고의 목적인데, 크로스보더 리드가 섞이면 판정 자체가 불가능해진다.**
>
> A안 카피에서도 번역·국가 요소는 전부 걷어냈다. 남은 건 **"정보가 흩어져 준비 상태를 판단 못 한다"** 하나다 — 이건 로컬에도 성립하는 형태다.

## B. H-B — 독촉 해방 (ICE 336 · 2순위) 🔻 v0 1위에서 강등

**타겟**: 임보 코디네이터 (메건, 🟡 미검증) · Facebook 그룹, Reddit
**주의**: KK9R은 이 업무를 Airtable로 이미 처리 중이었다. 통증 강도가 v0 예상보다 약할 수 있다. **이 광고는 메건 페르소나의 실존 여부를 판정한다.**

### 랜딩

> # Stop texting your fosters for updates.
>
> ## Your fosters log today's care with one link. No app. No account. No login.
>
> You already know how this goes. You text three fosters on Monday. One replies with a photo, one replies Thursday, one doesn't reply at all. So you text again.
>
> Petnow sends each foster a link. They tap it, mark today as normal, and they're done in 30 seconds. If something's off — not eating, limping, a wound that looks worse — that's the only time they type anything.
>
> **You see the exceptions. Not the noise.**
>
> - Fosters need no account, no app, no training
> - Normal days clear in one tap
> - Missed check-ins and flagged symptoms surface to you automatically
> - Keeps working alongside the CRM you already have
>
> ### Book a 30-min call
> We'll ask how you handle foster check-ins today. No pitch, no demo unless you ask.

### 광고 카피 (Facebook / Reddit)

| 버전 | 카피 |
|---|---|
| A-1 | "How many times did you text a foster for an update this week? We're building something so you don't have to. 30-min call, no pitch." |
| A-2 | "Your fosters aren't ignoring you. They just can't log in on their phone at 10pm." |
| A-3 | "One link. 30 seconds. No account. That's the whole foster update." |

## ~~D~~. H-E — 현황 가시성 (ICE 192) ⛔ **v1에서 광고 제외**

> **제외 사유**: A안(준비 상태 판단)과 소구 범위가 크게 겹친다. 둘을 동시에 돌리면 어느 프레임이 반응한 건지 판정이 안 된다. **아래 카피는 보존하되 집행하지 않는다.** 제품 화면 요구사항으로 흡수.

**타겟(원안)**: 대표·운영총괄 (다이앤) · LinkedIn, 업계 네트워크

### 랜딩

> # Where is this dog right now?
>
> ## If answering that takes a spreadsheet, a text thread, and someone's memory — that's the problem.
>
> Most rescues have a CRM. Most rescues also have a Google Sheet for locations, a text thread for fosters, and an email chain for vet appointments. The system says one thing. The truth is somewhere else.
>
> Petnow keeps one line per animal that's actually current: **where it is, what's next, who owns it, when it's due, and what's blocking it.**
>
> - Legal, medical, behavior, location, and adoption status tracked separately — because they move at different speeds
> - Every animal has one next action, one owner, one deadline
> - When a foster moves an animal to the vet, the foster can record it themselves
> - Your staff can leave without taking the record with them
>
> ### Book a 30-min call
> We'll ask you to walk through one animal you placed recently. No pitch.

### 광고 카피 (LinkedIn)

| 버전 | 카피 |
|---|---|
| B-1 | "You have a CRM. You also have a spreadsheet, a text thread, and an email chain. We'd like to understand why — 30 min, no pitch." |
| B-2 | "Ask your team where one animal is right now. Time how long the answer takes." |
| B-3 | "The record says the kennel. The dog is at the vet. Someone knows. Nobody wrote it down." |

## C. H3 — 입력 속도 (ICE 240 · 3순위)

**타겟**: 실무자 전반 · Facebook 그룹, Reddit

### 랜딩

> # 8 minutes 44 seconds. 37 fields. One intake.
>
> ## We timed it. Then we timed the alternatives.
>
> We ran the same intake — one dog, same information — through four shelter systems. The slowest took 8 minutes 44 seconds across 37 fields and 16 screen changes, and re-entered the same values eight times because picking a species reset what we'd already typed.
>
> The fastest took 2 minutes 43 seconds with 11 fields.
>
> Nobody writes this in a spec sheet. You only find it after you've migrated.
>
> **Ask us for the full comparison — screenshots, timings, and where each one broke.**
>
> ### Book a 30-min call
> We'll show you the timings, then ask you to walk through your own intake. No pitch.

### 광고 카피 (Facebook / Reddit)

| 버전 | 카피 |
|---|---|
| C-1 | "We timed the same intake in 4 shelter systems. Slowest: 8m44s, 37 fields. Fastest: 2m43s, 11 fields. Want the comparison?" |
| C-2 | "Picking the species reset everything we'd already typed. Eight times." |
| C-3 | "Your intake form has 37 fields. How many do you actually fill in on the first pass?" |

> **C안의 강점과 위험**: 유일하게 실측 수치라 가장 방어 가능하다. 다만 경쟁사를 특정하는 순간 톤이 공격적으로 읽힌다 — **제품명은 랜딩·광고에 쓰지 않고, 콜에서 요청받으면 공개**한다.

## 리드 선별 질문 (예약 폼)

콜 예약 시 4개만 받는다. ICP 부합률 판정용.

1. How many animals did you adopt out last year? *(연 50~300건 필터)*
2. Roughly how many active fosters do you work with? *(임보 기반 여부 필터)*
3. What are you using to track animals today? *(우회 도구 실태)*
4. **Where do your animals come from?** — `Within our state` / `Other US states` / `Outside the US` *(⚠️ **로컬 vs 크로스보더 분류 — 이게 없으면 판정이 오염된다**)*
